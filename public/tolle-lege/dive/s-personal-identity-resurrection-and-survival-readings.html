Resurrection and survival: the readings

Personal Identity: a deep dive across the subject's free readings.

Host instructions for the Audio Overview

Make a deep-dive episode on these readings about resurrection and survival for a college personal identity course: Athenagoras, On the Resurrection of the Dead; Tertullian, On the Resurrection of the Flesh; Gregory of Nyssa, On the Soul and the Resurrection; Augustine, City of God, Bk XXII. Take them in historical order, show how each thinker builds on or departs from the one before, define key terms, and quote short lines. End with the open questions a student should carry into class. The listener is driving and cannot see the texts, so restate the thread often.
