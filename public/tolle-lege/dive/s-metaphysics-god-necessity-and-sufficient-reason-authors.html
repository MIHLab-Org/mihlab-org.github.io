Host instructions for the Audio Overview

Make a deep-dive episode on how these Christian thinkers answer the question of god, necessity, and sufficient reason: Anselm of Canterbury, Thomas Aquinas, John Duns Scotus. For each, explain their view in their own terms, using their texts where the sources include them and the encyclopedia articles for background. Then compare: where they agree, where they split, and which view holds up best against the strongest objection. Treat each view at full strength before testing it.
