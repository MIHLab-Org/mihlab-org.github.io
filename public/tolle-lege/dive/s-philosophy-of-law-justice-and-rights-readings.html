Justice and rights: the readings

Philosophy of Law: a deep dive across the subject's free readings.

Host instructions for the Audio Overview

Make a deep-dive episode on these readings about justice and rights for a college philosophy of law course: Lactantius, Divine Institutes, Bks V-VI; Ambrose of Milan, De officiis; Thomas Aquinas, Summa theologiae II-II, qq. 57-58 (Right and Justice). Take them in historical order, show how each thinker builds on or departs from the one before, define key terms, and quote short lines. End with the open questions a student should carry into class. The listener is driving and cannot see the texts, so restate the thread often.
