Authority, tyranny, and the state: the authors' views

Philosophy of Law: a deep dive into what each thinker holds about authority, tyranny, and the state.

Host instructions for the Audio Overview

Make a deep-dive episode on how these Christian thinkers answer the question of authority, tyranny, and the state: Augustine, John of Salisbury, William of Ockham. For each, explain their view in their own terms, using their texts where the sources include them and the encyclopedia articles for background. Then compare: where they agree, where they split, and which view holds up best against the strongest objection. Treat each view at full strength before testing it. The encyclopedia articles are from 1913, so note where later scholarship has moved on. The listener is driving and cannot see the texts, so restate the thread often.
